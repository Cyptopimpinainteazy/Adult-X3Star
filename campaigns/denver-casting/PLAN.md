# Denver casting campaign delivery plan

Prepared on 2026-11-27. **Local campaign page and draft copy only; nothing purchased, submitted to an ad platform or published externally.**

## Target and message

Target Denver, Colorado, United States using the chosen platform's actual city-selection control. Keep this first campaign limited to the requested city; do not substitute all of Colorado or automatically expand into nearby cities. Geolocation is approximate, so applicants still report their own state. The landing page does not implement an IP geofence.

The audience is adults 18+ across genders and experience levels, researching adult creator/model work. The offer is a human-reviewed interest inquiry while x3STAR is in development, with a separate partner application option. It is not an announcement of a scheduled audition, a current paid production or guaranteed work.

Use the three variations and two owned-channel posts in [ADS.md](ADS.md). All point to `/casting/colorado/denver` with distinct attribution. Branded, non-graphic banner artwork can be prepared once the selected placement's actual dimensions and copy limits are known. No final image assets or ad-platform uploads are included in this pack.

## Paid channel candidate

TrafficJunky's current FAQ confirms city-level geographic selection. It also documents daily limits, scheduling and campaign approval requirements. This makes it a candidate for Denver delivery; it does not establish that x3STAR recruitment, the destination or Denver inventory will be approved. Review the full website and development-stage inquiry with the platform before submitting a campaign. [TrafficJunky FAQ](https://www.trafficjunky.com/resources/faq), [advertiser rules](https://www.trafficjunky.com/rules-and-regulations).

Do not assume that a plain-text recruitment page makes the wider site eligible for Google or Meta advertising. Google's sexual-content policy applies to ads and destinations, includes webcam entertainment among restricted content and excludes certain distribution networks. No mainstream-platform approval is claimed here. [Google sexual-content policy](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/6023699?hl=en).

The connected marketing service was checked read-only: no Google Ads, Meta Ads or social publishing connection was available for this project. A TrafficJunky advertiser account was not available through that connector. No external messages were sent to obtain approval.

## Attribution and review

The existing form stores `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign` and `utm_content` with a confirmed inquiry in the private Admin → Applications queue. The Denver entry path remains separate from the applicant's self-reported state. These are visitor-supplied attribution values; they do not independently prove an ad impression, a person's location or eligibility.

Use `denver_creator_casting` for the campaign and compare the three creative IDs after actual delivery. Report ad-platform spend/impressions/clicks separately from pending inquiry receipts, reviewed applicants and partner exits. An outgoing partner click is not a partner registration or revenue. No ad-network conversion pixel or postback was installed by this change.

The Denver page is a campaign destination with `noindex,follow`, a clean self-canonical and no organic sitemap entry. The developed Colorado guide and all existing journal SEO URLs remain unchanged. Paid delivery, public deployment and search indexing require separate live evidence.
