# Fifty industry posts for creator and partner recruitment

## Objective and editorial standard

User goal: write 50 industry blog posts to recruit models, attract affiliates and help people find collaborators and work. Add exactly 50 complete new original posts to the existing eight guides, retaining their URLs and content. Deliver 58 readable journal articles through the current Node/Express server, with bylines, maintained dates, descriptions, canonical metadata, sitemap entries and relevant next steps.

Each new post answers the distinct question in the table below. Aim for 650–850 original body words, at least 600 where the subject merits it, four or five developed sections, a concrete example or usable workflow, and two or three relevant related-article links. Avoid padding to meet a length target. Examples must be labeled illustrative; never invent testimonials, case-study results, average earnings, jobs, platform verification or ranking guarantees. No explicit sexual material, copied competitor content, pressure to participate, promises of acceptance or unverified program rates.

Audience values are `creators`, `affiliates` and `hiring`. New records use the existing article schema plus `audience`, `collection: 'industry-50'` and optional `sources:[{title,url}]`. Use `author: 'x3STAR Editorial'`, publication/modification date `2026-11-27`, accurate reading time, a unique lowercase slug, a concise description, `sections:[{heading,paragraphs,list?}]`, and `related:[slug,...]`. Article prose is plain text; the renderer owns HTML and links.

Current product facts: `/casting#apply` submits a creator inquiry to Admin review; `/casting#partners` offers separate active partner creator applications. `/talent` is public discovery; `/opportunities` contains free profile-board hiring/availability; `/jobs` holds reviewed paid employer ads, and `/employers` is the management portal. Inquiries do not guarantee work. The app is locally running; native streaming, live card processing and production deployment are not proven. Do not promise features merely because an old demo page mentions them. A new `/partners` publisher-inquiry page will reuse the existing `kind:'agent'` application queue; it will not promise approval, a contract or automatic commissions.

Primary research checked on 2026-11-27: [Google people-first guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content), [Google scaled-content policy](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies#scaled-content), [FTC disclosure guidance](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/disclosures-101-social-media-influencers), [FTC endorsement questions](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/ftcs-endorsement-guides-what-people-are-asking). Draw only short factual points from sources, paraphrase sparingly, and write original practical guidance. Do not quote sources or turn general planning into legal/tax advice. No keyword-volume or ranking claim has been verified.
